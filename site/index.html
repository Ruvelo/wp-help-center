Answers people find on their own.

A free help center for WordPress: instant search, “Was this helpful?”, docs-style articles, and Markdown, llms.txt and abilities so AI agents can read it too. No pro version, no upsells, no tracking.

Try it live in WordPress Playground → Read the docs

Answers as they type

Suggestions from titles and text, highlighted, keyboard and screen-reader friendly. An exact title goes straight to the article.

Articles that read like good docs

The help menu, breadcrumbs, an “Updated” date and an “On this page” outline. Related articles and a contact box underneath.

Hear what’s missing

One vote per reader, no cookies, and a short comment after a “No”, collected on one screen.

See what works

Views and the share of readers who found each article helpful, right in the article list.

Ready for AI agents, out of the box

WordPress left a knowledge store for AI agents to plugins. Every article is Markdown at its address plus .md, /llms.txt maps the help center, and abilities let agents search, read and (if you allow it) draft. With the MCP Adapter active, the help center has its own MCP server.

GET /wp-json/wp-abilities/v1/abilities/ruvelo-help-center/search-articles/run?input[query]=refund

wp help-center import ./docs --status=published
wp help-center llms

Our promises

  • No nags or upsells. No ads, review requests or “pro” teasers, and nothing outside the plugin’s own screens.
  • No tracking. No telemetry and no remote requests: no fonts, scripts or images from anywhere.
  • Never sold. Distributed only through WordPress.org and Composer, updated by WordPress itself.
  • Your content stays yours. Deleting the plugin keeps everything unless you ask otherwise.
